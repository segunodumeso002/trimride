# TrimRide Financial Ask Sheet

## Raise Objective
TrimRide is raising a pre-seed or seed round to move from validated MVP to launch-ready operations with measurable pilot growth.

## Why Capital Now
Core two-sided workflows are already working.
Funding is needed for hardening, market execution, and repeatable growth.

## Proposed Use of Funds (12 Months)
- 35% Product engineering and reliability hardening
- 25% Payments and compliance implementation
- 20% Pilot city launch and growth execution
- 10% QA, release automation, and device test coverage
- 10% Operations, customer support, and contingency

## Delivery Milestones
## Milestone 1 (0-3 Months)
- Production-grade payment flow live
- Error monitoring and alerting baseline deployed
- Release pipeline and incident runbook operational

## Milestone 2 (3-6 Months)
- Pilot city launch in focused zones
- Supply onboarding playbook active
- Service-level metrics tracked weekly

## Milestone 3 (6-12 Months)
- Retention and repeat-booking improvements
- Unit economics dashboard in place
- Expansion readiness for second market

## KPI Targets For Investors
- Request-to-accept rate
- Booking completion rate
- 30-day customer repeat rate
- Barber weekly active retention
- Gross booking value growth by zone
- Time-to-match and time-to-arrival improvements

## What We Already Validate Today
- Two mobile apps running: customer and barber
- Authentication and role-based sessions
- Booking request and response flow
- Real-time booking status updates
- Backend and database health workflow
- Push token and notification delivery path validated
